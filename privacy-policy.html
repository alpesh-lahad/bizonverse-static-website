Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

BIZONVERSE ("we", "us" or "our") respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used, disclosed and protected when you visit our website or contact us about our services.

Please read this policy together with any notices or choices presented to you when you use our website.

1. Information We May Collect

Information you provide

When you contact us through our website, you may provide information such as your name, email address, selected service, message and any other details you choose to share.

Website and device information

Depending on the technologies enabled on our website, information may include your IP address, browser and device details, approximate location, pages visited, referring website, interactions and visit timestamps.

Cookies and identifiers

Cookies, pixels, tags and similar technologies may store or access information on your device and help recognise browsers, measure website activity or support advertising. The information collected depends on the tools configured and your privacy choices.

2. How We Use Information

Information may be used, where applicable, to:

  • Respond to enquiries and communicate with you.
  • Understand which website pages and services are useful.
  • Measure website traffic and marketing performance.
  • Measure advertising effectiveness and conversions.
  • Personalise or manage advertising where enabled and permitted.
  • Maintain website security, prevent misuse and troubleshoot issues.
  • Meet applicable legal obligations.

We aim to use information only for appropriate purposes and in accordance with applicable law and the choices available to you.

3. Analytics and Advertising

Our website may use Google Analytics, Google Ads, Meta Pixel or similar tools to understand visitor activity, measure campaigns and evaluate advertising performance.

Depending on the implementation and your settings, these providers may receive information such as page URLs, device or browser details, online identifiers, interactions and information about visits or ad activity. Providers may use cookies or similar technologies for measurement, attribution, audience creation or advertising.

These services process information under their own applicable privacy terms and policies. Their actual collection and use depend on the features we enable, your settings and applicable requirements.

You can review Google's information about how it uses data from websites that use its services: How Google uses information from partner sites.

You can also review Google's Privacy Policy and Meta's Privacy Policy.

4. Cookies and Your Choices

Cookies and similar technologies may be used for essential website functions, analytics, advertising measurement or other purposes, depending on the technologies installed.

Where required, non-essential cookies and tracking technologies should only be activated after the appropriate consent has been obtained. You should be able to make or change relevant choices through the website's consent controls where provided.

You can also manage or delete cookies through your browser settings. Blocking cookies may affect some website features and may not automatically disable every form of measurement or advertising technology.

5. Third-Party Services and Links

Our website may include links, embedded content or integrations from third parties, such as video, maps, communication or analytics providers.

When you interact with those services, they may collect information under their own terms and privacy policies. We do not control the independent privacy practices of third-party websites or services.

6. When Information May Be Shared

Information may be shared with service providers that help operate our website, process enquiries, measure performance, deliver advertising, maintain security or provide technical support.

Information may also be disclosed when required by law, to respond to lawful requests, protect rights and safety, or support a legitimate business transfer where applicable.

We do not describe information as anonymous or promise that it is never shared unless the relevant practices have been verified.

7. Data Retention and Security

We intend to retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, subject to applicable legal, operational and security requirements.

Actual retention periods may vary by information type and service provider. We use appropriate safeguards intended to protect information, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed completely secure.

8. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on applicable law and your circumstances, you may have rights to request access to, correction of, erasure of or information about the processing of your personal data, or to withdraw consent where processing relies on consent.

You may contact us to make a privacy-related request. We may need to verify your identity and may be unable to fulfil some requests where an exception applies under applicable law.

If you are in India, requests will be handled in accordance with applicable Indian data protection law and any provisions in force at the relevant time.

9. Children's Privacy

Our website and services are intended for businesses and general audiences, not specifically for children. We do not knowingly seek to collect children's personal data in circumstances where doing so would be unlawful. If you believe a child has provided personal data to us, please contact us.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when our website, services, technologies or legal obligations change. The revised version will be published on this page with an updated date where appropriate.

11. Contact Us

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or want to make a privacy-related request, please contact BIZONVERSE through our Contact page.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in your initial enquiry unless it is necessary.
